BY THE COURT : 



       The petitioners who are presently undergoing their

Basic   Training         Course     (BSTC)/B.Ed.,           submitted      their

applications        in        pursuant       to     advertisement          dated

31.5.2008.        As       per        Note-2        appended          to     the

advertisement,            applicants              holding         professional

qualification diploma/certificate in Nursery Teachers

Training     PTT or Pre-Primary Teachers Training                            are

permitted to participate in process of selection with

the condition that till they acquire the training of

BSTC/B.Ed. or Bridge Course                   as the case may be, if

they be selected will be appointed with a stipend                             of

Rs.2750/-     and will get appointment on attaining the

training or bridge course as per Rules of 2008.



        The    case        of     the      petitioners         is     that     the

respondents       are         permitting           the      persons        having

certificate of NTT or Pre Primary Teachers Training

Course then there is no just and valid reason to deny

the   same   to     the       persons       like    petitioners       who    are

undergoing BSTC/B.Ed. or who have already appeared in

BSTC Examination but have awarded supplementary.                              As
                                  8 

per the petitioners the act of the respondents is a

discrimination and that is in violation of  Article 14 

and  16  of the Constitution of India.
 
 

        The controversy involved in these petitions has

already been adjudicated by this Court in a batch of

writ petitions decided on 11.9.2008 alongwith SBCivil

Writ Petition No.8232/2008 (Purshottam Mehta &amp; 37 Ors.

Vs. State of Raj. &amp; Ors.). While rejecting all those

petitions, a specific finding is given by this Court

in   the    case   aforesaid   that    the     applicants     holding

professional       qualification      under     NCTE   Regulations,

2001 constitutes a separate class and therefore the

qualification made under the Rules of 2008 is having

reasonable     nexus    with   the      object     sought     to   be

achieved.
 
 

        The    present    petitions      for      writ   also    stand

dismissed in light of the judgment referred above.



                                                ( GOVIND MATHUR ),J.
