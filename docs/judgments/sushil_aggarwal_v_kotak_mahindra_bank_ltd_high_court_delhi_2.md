## BORROWER'S CLAIM

This judgment does not involve a SARFAESI Act enforcement or a borrower's challenge to such action. There is no borrower's claim under Section 17 of the SARFAESI Act. The dispute was a civil suit for declaration of property shares between family members, which was resolved through a private Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) dated 25.11.2021. No procedural defect in demand notice, possession, auction, or reply under SARFAESI was alleged.

## HOLDING SUMMARY

This judgment does not establish any legal principle under the SARFAESI Act 2002 or the Security Interest (Enforcement) Rules 2002. It pertains to a private civil dispute between co-owners regarding the division of jointly held properties, resolved through a family settlement. The court merely recorded the settlement and disposed of the suit accordingly. No statutory interpretation of SARFAESI provisions was undertaken, and no precedent on enforcement procedure, auction validity, or borrower rights was set. This applies when: a property dispute between co-owners is resolved through a private settlement and the court gives effect to it without adjudicating on SARFAESI compliance.

## KEY FACTS OF THIS CASE

The case involved a family dispute between two brothers, Shri Sushil Aggarwal and Shri Vijay Aggarwal, sons of late Shri O.P. Gupta, over the ownership and possession of multiple jointly acquired properties in Delhi and surrounding areas. A civil suit (CS(OS) 26/2019) was filed for declaration of shares and partition. An interim status quo order was passed in January 2019. The parties later entered into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on 25.11.2021, dividing the properties between them. The suit was brought before the court for formal disposal in terms of the MoU, which the court approved and recorded as the basis for decree.

## WHAT THE COURT DECIDED

The Delhi High Court approved the private settlement (MoU) between the parties and disposed of the civil suit (CS(OS) 26/2019) in terms of the MoU. The court directed that the decree sheet be prepared accordingly. No relief was granted in favor of either party beyond giving legal effect to the private agreement. All pending applications were also disposed of.

## KEY QUOTE

The said MoU is in accordance with law. The Plaintiffs and the Defendants state that they have entered into the settlement at their own will and without any coercion, duress and undue influence and they have agreed to abide by the terms of the MoU.

## CONDITION: WHEN THIS JUDGMENT APPLIES

Applies when the dispute involves a private family settlement of property shares between co-owners without SARFAESI enforcement action.

## CONDITION: WHEN THIS JUDGMENT DOES NOT APPLY

1. When the dispute involves a SARFAESI enforcement action by a bank or financial institution — in that case, judgments like Kanaiyalal, Celir LLP, or Muthurathinasabathy apply.
2. When the borrower challenges a demand notice, possession, or auction under Section 17 — this judgment offers no legal principle on such challenges.
3. When the issue is about NPA classification, reply under Section 13(3A), or auction validity — this case is irrelevant.
